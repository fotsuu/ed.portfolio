Background & Engineering Focus

I am an Information Technology graduate from Davao del Sur State College (DSSC) based in Digos City, Philippines. I specialize in developing modern web applications, scalable database architectures, and AI integration workflows.

My engineering experience includes developing deployed production GovTech software for the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR-CENRO), where I built the Land Inventory & RLTA platform to automate barangay metrics, lot calculations, and Excel data ingestion across Davao del Sur.

As lead developer for FeedWise, I engineered an algorithmic poultry feed formulation system compliant with PhilSAN nutritional standards, combining real-time least-cost balancing simulations with cross-platform web and mobile interfaces.
