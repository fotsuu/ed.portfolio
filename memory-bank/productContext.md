# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists
In a competitive tech landscape, standard resumes and generic portfolio templates fail to convey real engineering depth. This portfolio acts as a living proof-of-work showcasing systems solving actual domain problems in government administration and agricultural feed science.

## Problems Solved
- **Shallow Portfolio Syndrome**: Replaced shallow todo-app demos with detailed technical breakdowns of real production challenges:
  - **DENR Land Inventory System**: Public-sector record management, title verification tracking, and spatial data indexing.
  - **FeedWise Platform**: Optimization algorithms balancing amino acids, crude protein, and cost constraints for poultry farmers.
- **Slow Load Times & Bloat**: Eliminates heavy frontend frameworks; uses streamlined vanilla web technologies for instant page loads and high Lighthouse scores.
- **Theme Inconsistency**: Provides a first-class dark/night mode with persistent preferences and automated system detection.

## Target Audience & Personas
1. **Technical Recruiters & Hiring Managers**: Seeking full-stack developers, AI workflow builders, and database specialists who understand business context and deliver reliable software.
2. **GovTech & Enterprise Clients**: Looking for engineers experienced in compliance, document workflows, and municipal/state agency digitalization.
3. **AgTech & Startup Collaborators**: Seeking engineers capable of implementing domain algorithms and optimization models.

## User Experience Principles
- **Instant Response**: Immediate interaction feedback via toast alerts, smooth page scrolling, and magnetic hover micro-animations.
- **Inclusive Accessibility**: Skip navigation links, ARIA labels, focus traps in modals, keyboard arrow navigation for image galleries, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Transparency**: Clear contact flow where input is kept safe, and the visitor reviews their pre-composed draft before sending.
